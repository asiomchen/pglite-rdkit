This site is a static documentation site run by Anton Siomchen
([anton.siomchen+pglite@gmail.com](mailto:anton.siomchen+pglite@gmail.com)). It has no accounts, no
cookies and no advertising. It doesn't sell your data or share it with anyone, apart from the
hosting provider described under [Hosting](#hosting).

## What Runs in Your Browser

The [Playground](/playground/), the [REPL](/repl/) and the demo on the home page run Postgres and
RDKit entirely in your browser. **The queries you write and the tables you create never leave your
device**, not even for analytics.

The site keeps a few things in your browser's storage. They stay on your device and are never
sent anywhere:

- **REPL database**: the tables you create in the REPL, so they're still there next time
- **REPL history and settings**: the commands you ran (for ↑/↓) and your `\x` and `\timing` choices
- **Analytics opt-out**: a flag, set only if you turn analytics off below

**Reset database** on the REPL page deletes your tables. Clearing this site's data in your browser
deletes all of the above.

## Analytics

The site uses a self-hosted instance of [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused analytics
tool, to count visits and see which parts of the site get used. It doesn't use cookies or store
anything on your device, and it doesn't build a profile of you or follow you to other sites.

For each page view, Umami records:

- the page address and the page you came from (the referrer)
- your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language
- your country, derived from your IP address

Umami doesn't store your IP address. It uses it for the country lookup, and combines it with your
browser details and a secret value into an anonymous visitor ID. That secret value is replaced at
the start of every month, so visits can be linked within a calendar month but not across months.

The site also sends these events about how it's used:

- **Links**: which external link was clicked (GitHub, npm, RDKit, PGlite and others), where on the
  page it was, and which page you were on
- **Queries**: that a query ran in the Playground, REPL or home-page demo, whether it succeeded,
  how long it took, and which built-in example it was
- **Buttons**: starting the home-page demo, resetting the Playground, resetting the
  REPL database, cancelling a REPL query, and opening a table from the REPL's table list
- **REPL time**: how long the REPL page was open and how many commands were run
- **Database start**: how long the in-browser database took to start, or the error if it failed

:::note[Your queries are never sent]
The SQL you write, the molecules in it and the results it returns are **never** sent to Umami or
anywhere else. The events above only say that a query ran, not what it was.
:::

The legal basis is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): knowing which pages and features
are used helps decide what to improve, and the data collected is minimal and not tied to you.

Analytics data is kept for 12 months and then deleted.

### Opting Out

You can turn analytics off for this site in this browser. The choice is saved in your browser's
storage, so it's lost if you clear this site's data.

<AnalyticsOptOut />

Ad and tracker blockers that block Umami work too.

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address
and request details to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse; see
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Your Rights

Because analytics data isn't linked to you, it usually can't be found or deleted for one person.
You still have the rights the GDPR gives you, including to ask about, object to or complain about
how your data is processed, and to complain to your data protection authority. For privacy
questions or requests, email
