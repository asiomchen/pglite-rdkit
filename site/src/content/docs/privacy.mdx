_Last updated 22 October 2026_

This site is a static documentation site run by Anton Siomchen. It has no accounts, no
cookies and no advertising, and it doesn't share data with anyone else.

## What Runs in Your Browser

The [Playground](/playground/), the [REPL](/repl/) and the demo on the home page run Postgres and
RDKit entirely in your browser. **The queries you write and the tables you create never leave your
device**, not even for analytics. The REPL keeps its database and command history in your browser's storage so they're
still there next time. **Reset database** on the REPL page, or clearing this site's data in your
browser, deletes them.

## Analytics

The site uses a self-hosted instance of [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused analytics
tool, to count visits and see which parts of the site get used. It doesn't use cookies or store
anything on your device, and it doesn't build a profile of you or follow you to other sites.

For each page view, Umami records:

- the page address and the page you came from (the referrer)
- your browser, operating system, device type, screen size and language
- your country, derived from your IP address

Your IP address is used for the country lookup and to tell visits apart, and is then discarded.
Umami combines it with your browser details and a salt that changes regularly into a value that
can't be traced back to you or linked across days.

The site also sends a few events about how it's used:

- which external links are clicked (GitHub, npm, RDKit, PGlite and others)
- that a query ran in the Playground, REPL or home-page demo, whether it succeeded and how long it
  took, and which built-in example it was
- how long the REPL was open and how many commands were run
- how long the in-browser database took to start, or the error if it failed

:::note[Your queries are never sent]
The SQL you write, the molecules in it and the results it returns are **never** sent to Umami or
anywhere else. The events above only say that a query ran, not what it was.
:::

The legal basis is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR): knowing which pages and features
are used helps decide what to improve, and the data collected is minimal and not tied to you.

### Opting Out

You can turn analytics off for this site in this browser. The choice is saved in your browser's
storage, so it's lost if you clear this site's data.

<AnalyticsOptOut />

Ad and tracker blockers that block Umami work too.

## Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address
and request details to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse; see
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Your Rights

Because analytics data isn't linked to you, it usually can't be found or deleted for one person.
You still have the rights the GDPR gives you, including to ask about, object to or complain about
how your data is processed, and to complain to your data protection authority. For questions,
[open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/asiomchen/pglite-rdkit/issues).
